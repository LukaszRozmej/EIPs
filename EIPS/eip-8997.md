## Abstract

This proposal adds the `STATEGASLEFT` instruction, which pushes the current `state_gas_reservoir` of [EIP-8037](./eip-8037.md). With `GAS` and `STATEGASLEFT` read before and after a call, a contract can compute all the gas that the call cost the sender, including the state-gas that the reservoir paid.

## Motivation

Under [EIP-8037](./eip-8037.md), `GAS` returns `gas_left` only. State-gas that the reservoir pays does not appear in `gasleft()` differences. Contracts that bill a sub-call for the gas it used, such as an [ERC-4337](./eip-4337.md) `EntryPoint`, therefore do not bill state creation that the reservoir pays. The sender pays it instead.

A contract cannot read the reservoir today, so it cannot fix this. This proposal exposes the reservoir. It changes no cost and no result of any other instruction.

## Specification

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `STATEGASLEFT` opcode | `0x4c` (provisional) |
| `STATEGASLEFT` gas cost | 2 |

`STATEGASLEFT` takes no input from the stack. It pushes the value of `state_gas_reservoir` of [EIP-8037](./eip-8037.md) at the time of the instruction, after its own charge. Its cost is execution-gas.

## Rationale

### The reservoir, not the state-gas used

Under [EIP-8037](./eip-8037.md), state-gas comes from the reservoir first and from `gas_left` when the reservoir is empty. The part that comes from `gas_left` already appears in `gasleft()` differences. An instruction that returned the total state-gas used (`evm_state_gas_used`) would make a contract that adds it to a `gasleft()` difference count that part twice. The reservoir is the other half of what the sender pays: the gas that the transaction used before refunds is `tx.gas - gas_left - state_gas_reservoir`. So, for reads of `GAS` and `STATEGASLEFT` in the same frame before and after a call:

```python
call_cost = (gas_before - gas_after) + (reservoir_before - reservoir_after)
```

`call_cost` is all the gas that the call cost the sender before refunds: the call's own charges, the execution-gas of the callee and the state-gas, from either pool.

### The value can increase

Under [EIP-8037](./eip-8037.md), refills and rollbacks credit the reservoir. Thus the reservoir can be higher after a call than before it, for example when the callee clears a slot that an earlier frame created. A contract that bills by these differences must use signed arithmetic and choose how to treat a negative amount, for example as no charge, or as a credit to the call that removed the state.

### A transaction-wide value

The reservoir is one value for the whole transaction. Inside a child frame, `STATEGASLEFT` returns what is left of the shared reservoir, not an amount that belongs to the frame.

## Backwards Compatibility

`0x4c` was an undefined instruction before. A contract that executed it halted exceptionally. No deployed contract can depend on this, except by failing on purpose.

## Test Cases

- In a transaction with `tx.gas` above `TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT`, `STATEGASLEFT` returns `tx.gas - TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT` at the first instruction, less the state-gas of the pre-execution phase that the reservoir paid.
- In a transaction with `tx.gas <= TX_MAX_GAS_LIMIT`, it returns 0 at the first instruction.
- Around a call whose callee creates one new slot paid by the reservoir, `reservoir_before - reservoir_after` is the state-gas of the slot minus its legacy share, and `call_cost` is the total gas of the call.
- Around a call whose callee clears a slot that the caller created earlier in the transaction, the difference of `STATEGASLEFT` is negative.

## Security Considerations

`STATEGASLEFT` lets a contract learn whether the transaction has a reservoir, and how large it is. A contract could already learn most of this from `GAS` and `tx.gas`. A contract that changes its behavior based on the reservoir gives the sender control over that behavior, as for any value that depends on `tx.gas`.

The reservoir is shared. A value that a contract reads before a call does not guarantee that the reservoir still holds it when a later call needs it, because an earlier callee can use it.
